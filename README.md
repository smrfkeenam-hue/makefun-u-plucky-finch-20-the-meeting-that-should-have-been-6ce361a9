One line, 136 characters, that makes a meeting-that-should-have-been-an-email funny:

> I attended a meeting about an email, where we agreed the email needed a meeting, and the minutes were emailed to everyone who was there.

The joke is the loop: a meeting about an email decides the email needs a meeting, and the minutes travel back by email to the people who were already in the room. No setup, no explanation — the twist lands in the final clause.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with no trailing newline.
